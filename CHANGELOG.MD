# Changelog

## v1.1.0

* Refactoring for improved performance
* Added information on the effects of controls and assumptions to the Result Summary sheet
* Bug fix: inline assumptions and countermeasures defined in the Risk Treatment sheet sometimes had incorrect IDs. (Inline assumptions / countermeasures are assumptions / countermeasures defined in a comment)
* Moved the definitions and catalogs to the right to put more focus on the ""active"" sheets
* Added a column for 4TD IDs to the Assumptions sheet and the Result Summary sheet
* Changed the name of the Result Summary sheet to Result to reduce ambiguity
* Refined the wording of QI1 and the wording of its follow-up question for better consistency
* On the Documentation sheet: Changed Release to Version for the sake of simplicity
* QI15 now belongs to Function and Availability in the Questionnaire
* Changed the wording of the second question for QA5 to reduce ambiguity
* Added a second question to QI6 for more clarity regarding (non-)safety products
* Added a Question Names column to the Assumptions and the Countermeasures sheet for better handling of the IDs
* The questionnaire now displays headings for the columns on the sheet
* Added a quality indicator for cases where QI14 is rated lower than QI7 (loss of integrity vs. loss of availability)
* The quality indicator for ""support time shorter than product lifetime"" no longer uses the update strategy as an input
* Changed the indentation of the questions to better match the current structure of the questionnaire
